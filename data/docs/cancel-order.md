title: Cancelling an order
date: 2026-09-20
---

You can cancel an order at any time before it is dispatched.

**How to cancel**
1. Sign in to the ShopEase customer portal and open **My Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Cancel Order**.
3. Choose a reason and confirm.

You see a confirmation message on screen and receive a confirmation email. The order status changes to **Cancelled**.

**Refunds for cancelled orders**
If you already paid, the full amount is refunded to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of the cancellation. For cash on delivery orders, nothing was paid, so no refund is needed.

**If the order is already dispatched**
Cancellation is no longer possible, and **Cancel Order** is not shown. You can refuse the delivery or return the items within 30 days under the refund policy.

If you cannot cancel online, contact customer support on +91 00000 00000 or support@shopease.example (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM) and quote your order number.
