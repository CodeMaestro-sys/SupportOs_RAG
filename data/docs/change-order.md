title: Changing an order
date: 2026-09-15
---

You can change an order until it is dispatched. Once the order status shows **Dispatched**, it can no longer be changed.

**What you can change**
- Item size or colour
- Quantity
- Delivery address
- Delivery option, where the new option is available

**How to change an order**
1. Sign in to the ShopEase customer portal and open **My Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Change Order**.
3. Make your change and click **Save**. You receive an updated confirmation email.

**Timing:** orders placed before 12 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day, so make changes as soon as possible after ordering.

**If the order is already dispatched**
You cannot change it. Once it is delivered, you can return items within 30 days under the refund policy.

If **Change Order** is not shown for your order, or the change you need is not listed, contact customer support on +91 00000 00000 or support@shopease.example (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM) and quote your order number.
