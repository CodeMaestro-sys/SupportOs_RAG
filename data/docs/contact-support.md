title: Contacting ShopEase customer support
date: 2026-09-01
---

ShopEase customer support can be reached in three ways:

- **Phone:** +91 00000 00000. Your call goes directly to a support agent.
- **Email:** support@shopease.example. We reply within 1 business day.
- **Live chat:** available on www.shopease.example. Choose "Talk to an agent" to be connected to a person.

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day.

To help us find your case quickly, have your order number (for example ORD-12345) or your registered email address ready. If your question is about an invoice, also include the invoice number.

If you prefer to speak to a human agent instead of using the automated help, use the phone line or the "Talk to an agent" option in live chat. The ShopEase customer portal also has a Help Center with answers to common questions, available at any time.
