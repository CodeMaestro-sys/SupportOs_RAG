title: How long delivery takes
date: 2026-10-05
---

Delivery times depend on the option you choose at checkout.

| Option | Delivery time |
|---|---|
| Standard delivery | 4 to 6 business days |
| Express delivery | 1 to 2 business days |

**How the time is counted**
- The time starts on the day your order is dispatched, not the day you place it.
- Orders placed before 12 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 12 PM are dispatched the next business day.
- Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays.

Deliveries to remote areas can take up to 2 additional business days. The estimated delivery date is shown on your order page under **My Orders** and in your dispatch email.

**If your order is late**
If your order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, check **Track Order** first. If there is no update, contact customer support on +91 00000 00000 or support@shopease.example (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM) and quote your order number.
