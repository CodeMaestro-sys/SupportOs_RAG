You can update your personal details at any time in the ShopEase customer portal.

**How to edit your details**
1. Sign in to the ShopEase customer portal.
2. Open **Account Settings** and select **Profile**.
3. Change your name, mobile number or email address.
4. Click **Save**. Changes apply immediately.

**Changing your email address:** we send a 6-digit verification code to the new address. Your email only changes after you enter the code. Order updates and invoices go to the new address from then on.

**Changing your password:** go to **Account Settings**, then **Password and Security**.

**Delivery addresses:** these are managed separately under **Saved Addresses** in Account Settings (see the shipping address guide).

If a detail cannot be edited online, for example a name that is locked after an order has been placed, contact customer support on +91 00000 00000 or support@shopease.example (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM).
