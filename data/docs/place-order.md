title: Placing an order
date: 2026-08-12
---

To place an order you need a ShopEase account. You can create one for free on www.shopease.example.

**Steps**
1. Add items to your cart and click **Checkout**.
2. Confirm or choose your delivery address.
3. Select a delivery option: standard, express, same-day (where available) or store pickup.
4. Choose a payment method and click **Place Order**.

After you place the order you see a confirmation page and receive a confirmation email. The email contains your order number (for example ORD-12345). The order also appears under **My Orders** in the ShopEase customer portal.

**Dispatch timing:** orders placed before 12 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 12 PM are dispatched the next business day.

If an item is out of stock, it cannot be added to the cart. If your payment fails, see the payment problems guide. For any other problem, contact customer support on +91 00000 00000 or support@shopease.example (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM).
