ShopEase offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. You can return an eligible item within 30 days of delivery for a refund.

**Eligible items**
- Unused items in their original packaging
- Items that arrived damaged or defective
- Items that are wrong or different from your order
- Orders that never arrived

**How to request a refund**
1. Sign in to the ShopEase customer portal and open **My Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Return or Refund**.
3. Choose the item and the reason, and submit the request.

We email you once the request is approved. For returns, approval happens after we receive and check the item.

**Refund time**
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. For returns, this starts when the refund is approved. For cancelled orders, it starts on the day of cancellation. The same timeline applies to refunds for duplicate or unauthorized charges, damaged or defective items, and orders that did not arrive.

For questions about a refund, contact customer support on +91 00000 00000 or support@shopease.example (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM).
